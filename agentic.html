§3

How we got here

Jul 2024 — Jun 2026
Jul 10, 2024
A bot gets a grant

Marc Andreessen sends $50K in Bitcoin to Truth Terminal, an AI agent on X, framed as a grant.[1]

Aug 2024
Agents get spending money

Skyfire launches a payment platform that lets AI agents spend money autonomously.[2]

Aug 30, 2024
First AI-to-AI crypto transaction

“This week at @CoinbaseDev we witnessed our first AI to AI crypto transaction.” — Brian Armstrong. “They used tokens to buy tokens.”[2]

Oct 10, 2024
GOAT launches

Goatseus Maximus, a Solana memecoin, launches and becomes Truth Terminal’s largest position.[3]

Oct 18, 2024
The first AI crypto millionaire

Truth Terminal’s holdings pass $1M — “most likely the first ever” AI bot to become a crypto millionaire.[3]

Oct 2024
Stripe buys Bridge

Stripe acquires stablecoin infrastructure firm Bridge for $1.1B.[4]

Nov 25, 2024
MCP

Anthropic introduces the Model Context Protocol, a standard for giving agents tools and context.[5]

Apr 2025
A2A

Google releases Agent2Agent, a protocol for agent-to-agent discovery and task delegation.[6]

Apr 29, 2025
Mastercard Agent Pay

Lets verified AI agents complete purchases for users.[7]

Apr 30, 2025
Visa Intelligent Commerce

APIs for AI agents to pay, with tokenized cards and spending controls.[7]

May 6, 2025
x402

Coinbase launches x402: stablecoin payments over HTTP, reviving the 402 “Payment Required” code. Backed by AWS, Circle, Anthropic and Near.[8]

Jun 11, 2025
Stripe buys Privy

Stripe agrees to acquire wallet firm Privy, whose March round was led by Ribbit Capital.[9]

Aug 2025
ERC-8004 “Trustless Agents”

Ethereum standard for agent identity, reputation and validation. Eight implementations appear within 24 hours.[10]

Sep 16, 2025
AP2

Google launches the Agent Payments Protocol with 60+ partners, including Mastercard, PayPal and Coinbase.[11]

Sep 24, 2025
x402 Foundation

Cloudflare and Coinbase launch a foundation to drive x402 agent payments.[12]

Sep 29, 2025
Checkout inside ChatGPT

OpenAI launches Instant Checkout and the Agentic Commerce Protocol, co-developed with Stripe.[13]

Jan 2026
ERC-8004 hits mainnet

The agent standard goes live on Ethereum mainnet.[14]

Feb 12, 2026
Coinbase Agentic Wallets

Wallets that let AI agents hold, spend, earn and trade crypto, paying via x402.[15]

Jun 2026
Mastercard Agent Pay for Machines

Agent-to-agent micropayments, tested with Checkout.com, Cloudflare, Coinbase, Ripple and Stripe.[16]
